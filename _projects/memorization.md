title: Memorization versus generalization
description: Ongoing. Exploratory.
importance: 5
category: interpretability
---

Controlled noisy-label tasks, extended to ten pretrained LLMs, examine how repetition per context separates memorization from generalization. Findings so far include locality of errors to a token's own distractor label and selective suppression of memorization by training-time layer dropout. A separate Pythia study shows that apparent layer-removal robustness of memorized sequences is a confidence confound.
