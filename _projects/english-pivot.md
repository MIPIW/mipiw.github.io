With Logit Lens and layer-wise probing on multilingual LLMs, I study when English-dominant intermediate lexicalization preserves target-language distinctions (e.g. kinship terms, French fleuve/rivière, Korean 춥다/차갑다) and when it collapses them. Languages: Korean, English, French, Chinese, Japanese.
