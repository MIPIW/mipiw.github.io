Across 16 open-weight LLMs on MMLU and MMLU-Pro under perturbations, higher confidence is associated with shorter responses. The effect is localized to newly generated reasoning and supports workload forecasting for request scheduling. Related evaluation work argues that reasoning versus knowledge and format versus content are entangled in benchmarks.
