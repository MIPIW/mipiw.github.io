Phase 1 traces LAPE neurons onto multi-layer SAE latents in Pythia and BLOOM using weight-based and gradient-based attribution, validated with activation patching. Side findings concern late-layer outlier dimensions absorbed by a single dominant latent.

Phase 2 asks whether a layer's information storage strategy (concentration versus superposition) tracks its causal importance, testing the single-layer importance/sparsity trade-off along depth.
