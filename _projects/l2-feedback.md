On Korean middle-school English essays, I compare open-weight LLMs with a population of teachers in rubric-based scoring and formative feedback. Questions: how close do models come to human agreement and diversity, and does LLM feedback show the traits the L2 feedback literature flags as problematic? A chance-corrected locus metric automatically captures where feedback points in the essay.
